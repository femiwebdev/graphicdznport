How I work

I produce image and video content for event collateral and high-profile PR campaigns, working across Adobe Creative Suite — Photoshop, Illustrator and Premiere Pro — alongside Affinity, Canva and open-source animation tools.

Much of that work is collaborative brand strategy: promoting firm leadership and key industry figures, including Mr Atoyebi Oyetola, SAN, and building visibility for them across digital channels. It means I’m used to working to a house style, to a deadline, and with people whose names are on the work.

Briefs usually start with a short call, move to two or three directions, then to refinement and final files — source artwork included, so nothing is locked to me.
